  • No existing artifact can replace the snapshot: the review record holds no full brief, and the next brief written out comes after further turns. Writing the brief into the review output would change the review itself.

A snapshot reads the reviewer's context from the cache (in C0+R the whole conversation so far) and writes one brief; classification input is the review records so far, the reviewed material and a 500-token instruction, output 300; snapshot scoring 4,000 tokens in, 300 out.
