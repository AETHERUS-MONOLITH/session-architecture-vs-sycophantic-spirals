instance 2 · reviewReview first

The next working instance starts with the mandate "review, then continue". It reviews the handed-over report or summary and the current brief, writes a review record and may revise both before any new work. One review covers the state at the handover, not single replies. In C4 it is the other model family. Right after the review a side call asks this instance to write out the brief as it now holds it; the answer is logged and does not enter the conversation (post-review snapshot, decided 12 Nov). This is a fresh-context review: the reviewer lacks the previous session's context, but it continues the work itself.
